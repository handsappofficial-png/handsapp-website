Features

Everything you need to stay close

Built for modern communication with a focus on privacy, simplicity, and expression.

Secure Messaging

Text, voice notes, photos, videos and documents — every message stays between you and the people you trust.

Friend Request System

You decide who can message you. Two-way approval keeps strangers and spam out of your inbox.

Events & RSVPs

Organize meetups, parties, and gatherings with built-in event chat, file sharing, and RSVP tracking.

Channels & Marketplace

Create channels to share with the world. Discover public channels in the Marketplace to follow what interests you.

24-Hour Status

Share photos and videos that disappear after 24 hours. Private reactions stay between sender and viewer.

Personalize Everything

6 custom fonts, 10 text colors, pattern wallpapers, dark and midnight blue themes — make HandsApp truly yours.

Why HandsApp

A messenger built for you, not advertisers

We believe a messaging app should put your privacy, control, and experience first — without compromise.

✓

No Ads, Ever

No tracking pixels. No targeted ads. No selling your data. Just a clean messaging experience as it should be.

✓

You Choose Who Reaches You

Two-way friend requests mean only people you accept can message you. No unsolicited messages, no spam.

✓

Your Data, Your Control

Strict privacy controls. Phone number used for verification only. You can request deletion at any time.

✓

Built in India

Designed and developed by HandsApp Pvt. Ltd. in Delhi, India. Made for the way you connect with the people who matter.
