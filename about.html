About

What is HandsApp?

HandsApp is a modern cloud-native messaging platform designed to bring people closer. With secure messaging, group chats, events, channels, and status updates — HandsApp makes communication simple, expressive, and private.

Built with a focus on privacy and user experience, HandsApp features a unique two-way friend request system that gives you full control over who can message you. Create channels to share content with the world, organize events, customize your chat experience, and stay connected with the people who matter most.
